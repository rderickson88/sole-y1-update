Quality Matters

Quality Matters is a non-profit organization that provides rubrics, training, and asessment methology for measuring the effectiveness of online teaching.

Elms College has engaged in a relationship with Quality Matters to measure and enhance our online coursework.

If you are interested in learning more or becoming a Peer Reviewer on campus, please contact:

  • Ryan Erickson, Instructional Designer: ericksonr@elms.edu
  • Becky Lescarbeau: Education Technology Specialist: lescarbeaur@elms.edu

Professional Development @ Elms

Twice a Year, Instructional Design delivers professinonal development in the areas of online instructional strategies, assessment, and Quality Matters course alignment.

Speak with Ryan Erickson, Instructional Designer, if you are interested in a particular topic or looking for some expertise in a particular field.
